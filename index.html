Little rules

Iron that actually absorbs

  • Eat the guava, orange, mandarin or pomegranate with lunch.
  • No tea or coffee for 1 hour around meals.
  • Take iron and vitamin D with lunch, 2 hours away from yogurt, milk and cheese.

No added sugar

  • Tea with milk, no sugar. No iced coffee, juices or desserts.
  • Fruit is your sweet treat.
  • Skip ketchup, BBQ, teriyaki, oyster and sweet chili sauce.
  • Use low-sodium soy sauce, 1 to 2 tbsp per meal.

Butter, measured

  • 1 tsp on eggs and vegetables.
  • 2 tsp in mash or a soup.
  • Omelets get a small handful (30 g) of mozzarella.

Gym days

  • Banana, apple or 3 fresh dates 60 to 90 minutes before.
  • Dinner after the gym is your recovery meal; keep the full protein.

Supplements

  • Check your Centrum label; most contain iron and vitamin D already. Show the doctor all three labels.
  • Stop Centrum 2 to 3 days before blood tests; its biotin can distort thyroid results.
  • 10 to 20 minutes of midday sun, 3 to 4 times a week.
